MAGESH PORTFOLIO — QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html in your browser (or use the VS Code Live Server extension).
4. Replace hello@example.com with your real email address.
5. Replace the LinkedIn homepage link with your actual LinkedIn profile URL.
6. Add your real project screenshots and links when they are ready.

Files:
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: layout, colours, responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation and footer year

Important:
- The project cards are clearly labelled as concept work. Keep that label unless you have genuine client work and permission to show it.
- Civic communication is framed as neutral, factual public information.
- The city descriptions are research themes, not claims about market size or proven demand.
